<p align="center"><b>Fair, stewarded sim racing for Assetto Corsa and beyond.</b></p>

Digital Drivers is a racing league with its own platform and desktop app:

- **Digital Drivers Club** – the community every driver joins, free of charge.
- **Digital Drivers Cup** – the championship: scheduled races, series, standings and licences from Rookie to Platinum.
- **Digital Drivers Association** – the officials: race directors, stewards and scrutineers.

Races run on dedicated servers. Every car and track passes technical scrutineering before the start, results
become official after a protest period, and a fairness rating decides who may enter which grid.

## Open source

| Repository | What |
| --- | --- |
| [dd-desktop](https://github.com/DigitalDrivers/dd-desktop) | Desktop app for Windows: one-click race join, scrutineering, content install. The source is public so every driver can check what the app reads on their PC. |
| [dd-link](https://github.com/DigitalDrivers/dd-link) | AssettoServer plugin that reports results, laps and incidents to the platform. |

The platform itself is under active development. Website: digitaldrivers.club (coming soon).
